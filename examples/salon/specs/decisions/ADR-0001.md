# Closing a booked Slot

- Context: ACT-CloseSlot can break INV-001 when an Appointment holds the Slot.
- Options: (1) reject CloseSlot; (2) cancel the affected Appointments and notify; (3) allow it and flag conflicts for manual rescheduling.
- Decision: (1). The Stylist runs ACT-CancelAppointment first.
- Consequences: a Stylist cannot silently strand a Customer; closing a busy day costs one cancel per Appointment.
- Affects: REQ-006, INV-001, ACT-CloseSlot
